Space Events

The sky is always full of surprises. From eclipses to meteor showers, here are the most exciting events that skywatchers around the world look forward to.

Solar Eclipse

The Moon passes between the Sun and Earth, blocking sunlight.

Never look at it without special eclipse glasses.
Lunar Eclipse

Earth passes between the Sun and the Moon, casting its shadow.

The Moon can turn a deep red color, often called a "blood moon".
Meteor Showers

Streaks of light that appear when Earth passes through comet dust.

The Perseids peak in August and the Geminids in December.
Comets

Icy bodies that grow glowing tails as they near the Sun.

Halley's Comet returns about every 76 years, next in 2061.
Supermoon

A full Moon that appears bigger and brighter than usual.

It happens when the Moon is closest to Earth in its orbit.
