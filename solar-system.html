The Solar System

Our Solar System formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a giant cloud of gas and dust. At its center is the Sun, orbited by eight planets, dozens of moons, asteroids, and comets.

Sun

The star at the center of our Solar System, giving us light and heat.

Holds about 99.8% of the Solar System's total mass.
Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest to the Sun.

One year here lasts only 88 Earth days.
Venus

A planet wrapped in thick clouds of carbon dioxide.

The hottest planet, with surface temperatures around 465°C.
Earth

Our home and the only known planet with life.

About 71% of its surface is covered by water.
Mars

The Red Planet, colored by iron oxide on its surface.

Home to Olympus Mons, the tallest volcano in the Solar System.
Jupiter

The largest planet, a giant ball of gas.

Its Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than Earth.
Saturn

Famous for its bright rings of ice and rock.

Saturn is so light that it could float in water.
Uranus

An ice giant that spins on its side Uranus .

Its axis is tilted by about 98 degrees.
Neptune

The farthest planet, dark, cold, and windy.

Has the fastest winds in the Solar System.
