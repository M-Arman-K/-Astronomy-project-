Stars & Galaxies

Journey deep into cosmic space to explore how stars are born inside nebulae, witness supernova explosions, and discover billions of magnificent galaxies.

Nebula

Clouds of gas and dust where new stars are born.

Fact

Eagle Nebula hamse taqreeban 7,000 light-years door hai.

Supernova

The final explosion of a massive star, scattering new elements across space.

Fact

The Crab Nebula is the remaining debris of a supernova observed in the year 1054.

Spiral Galaxy

A collection of billions of stars rotating in a spiral shape.

Fact

Andromeda is located about 2.5 million light-years away from us.

Black Hole

A place where gravity is so intense that not even light can escape.

Fact

In 2019, the first-ever actual image of a black hole was captured by the Event Horizon Telescope.

Star Cluster

A cluster of stars that were all formed from the same original cloud.

Fact

The Pleiades cluster can easily be seen from Earth with the naked eye.

Milky Way

Our home galaxy, which contains our entire Solar System.

Fact

It is home to hundreds of billions of stars.
